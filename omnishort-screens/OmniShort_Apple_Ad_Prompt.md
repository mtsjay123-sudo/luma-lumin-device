# OmniShort: "The Quiet Edge"
### Prompt pack for a vertical (9:16) Apple-grade commercial · ends on Lumin Holdings

Written for **Google Veo 3.1** (best for realism and built-in sound). Also works with **Sora 2**, **Kling 2.x** and **Runway Gen-4**.

---

## 0. How to use this (read first)

1. **Make the character once, then reuse her.** Generate the reference image in §3 in an image model (Nano Banana / Gemini Image, Midjourney v7 or GPT-Image). Save the best one as `maya_ref.png`. Attach it to **every** shot ("Ingredients to Video" in Veo, "Character reference" in Kling/Runway). AI video forgets faces between clips unless you do this.
2. **One shot per generation.** Veo makes 8-second clips. Generate each shot in §5 on its own, in **9:16, 1080p**, then cut them together in CapCut, Premiere or Resolve.
3. **Paste the STYLE BIBLE (§2) at the top of every shot prompt.** Keeping the same lens, light and grade words is what makes 11 clips look like one film.
4. **Never let the AI draw the app's interface or any words.** It will garble them. Two ways to get the real OmniShort screens on the laptop:
   - **Best:** prompt the laptop screen as *"a softly glowing, out-of-focus amber interface"*. Then in your editor, corner-pin (screen-replace) the real screenshots from this folder onto it.
   - **Fast:** attach the matching screenshot (listed per shot) as a reference image and say *"the laptop display shows the attached interface, slightly out of focus"*. Keep the screen soft, so any warping hides.
5. **All titles go on in the edit, not in the AI.** Typography rules are in §7.
6. **Voiceover:** record the script in §6 with ElevenLabs. Suggested voices: a calm, low male or female narrator. Stability 45–55 %, Style 10–20 %. Turn off Veo's spoken dialogue for those shots and keep only its SFX/ambience.

## 1. The idea

Apple doesn't sell features; it sells a feeling, then proves it with the product. Bloomberg's look is authority: black, amber, numbers you trust.

**Story in one line:** before sunrise, one investor is drowning in noise. She opens OmniShort, and the noise turns into a framework: verified prices, ten thousand futures, her own notes chained together, a thesis built layer by layer. At the end, a calm check of the one variable no model can see: herself. Sunrise. Clarity. *Research, with a framework.*

**Emotional curve:** tension (cold, blue, noisy) → breath (silence, the laptop opens) → flow (warm amber, rhythm builds from keystrokes) → stillness ("You.") → release (sunrise, gold) → brand (black, gold hairline, Lumin Holdings).

## 2. STYLE BIBLE: paste at the top of every shot

```
STYLE: Premium Apple-style commercial, photoreal live action, vertical 9:16 frame composed for a phone screen.
Shot on ARRI Alexa 35 with Zeiss Supreme Prime lenses, 1.5x restraint: mostly 35mm, 50mm and 100mm macro, shallow depth of field (T2.0), creamy natural bokeh, no fisheye, no distortion.
Camera is calm and precise like a motion-control rig: slow push-ins, gentle lateral slides, locked-off frames, smooth rack focus. No handheld shake, no fast whip pans, no drone swoops.
Lighting: soft, directional, motivated. One large window or screen as the key, deep natural falloff into shadow, subtle edge/rim light separating subject from background, real practical lamps. Skin looks real: visible pores, natural texture, no plastic smoothing.
Color: restrained and expensive. Deep charcoal blacks (never crushed to pure black), cool slate-blue shadows early, warm amber-gold highlights (#F2B544) from screens and sunrise. Low saturation except the gold. Clean filmic contrast, very fine grain, gentle halation on highlights.
Set design: minimal, architectural, uncluttered. Matte surfaces, raw concrete, warm oak, brushed aluminium, floor-to-ceiling glass. Nothing cheap, no RGB gaming lights, no neon, no clutter.
Mood: quiet confidence, focus, intelligence. Every frame could be a poster.
Exclude: on-screen text, captions, logos, watermarks, subtitles, readable UI, extra fingers, warped hands, morphing faces, cartoon or CGI look, oversaturated colors, lens flares everywhere, sci-fi holograms, cheesy stock-footage smiles.
```

> Prefer a different lead? Swap the description (age, ethnicity, gender, hair, clothes), but keep **one** wardrobe item that's always visible (the charcoal sweater, the gold ring). The audience tracks the person by those details.

**Identity line, added to every shot that shows her:**
`Maya (use attached reference): 29-year-old Black woman, close-cropped natural hair, thin gold hoops, gold ring on right index finger, charcoal merino crewneck.`

**Hero object:** a slim space-gray aluminium laptop (MacBook-like but **unbranded**: no Apple logo).

## 4. Location

A minimal high-rise apartment above a big city before sunrise. Floor-to-ceiling windows, raw concrete wall, a long warm-oak desk with only: the laptop, a black ceramic cup of black coffee, an open paper notebook with handwritten notes, a fountain pen and her phone face-up. The city outside starts in deep blue hour and turns gold by the end.

## 7. Typography & end cards (add in the edit)

- **Font:** SF Pro Display / Inter / Schibsted Grotesk, Bold or Heavy, tight tracking (-3 %).
- **Style:** big, white, centered, **one to three words at a time**, synced to the voice: "Milliseconds." "On a feeling." "Two sources." "10,000 futures." "One chain." "Layer by layer." "You."
- **Accent:** one word per title in gold gradient (#FFF3CF → #F2B544 → #B97A12).
- **Safe zone for Reels/TikTok:** keep all text between 14 % and 70 % of the frame height, away from the caption and button area at the bottom and right.
- **Logo card (3 s):** pure black, OmniShort mark plus wordmark centered, "By Luminaire A.I." small in mono underneath.
- **End card (3 s):** pure black, a thin gold hairline that draws outward from the center, **LUMIN HOLDINGS** in widely spaced caps (+24 % tracking), "A Lumin Holdings company" small beneath. Slow 2-second fade out.

## 9. Quality checklist before you post

- [ ] Same face, sweater and ring in every shot (regenerate any that drift).
- [ ] Hands have five fingers; the ring is on the same hand.
- [ ] No fake text or Apple logo anywhere (the laptop is unbranded).
- [ ] Real OmniShort screenshots corner-pinned on the screen shots (5, 6, 7, 8, 11).
- [ ] Grade matched across clips: cool → warm across the film.
- [ ] Voice at about −14 LUFS; music ducks under the voice.
- [ ] Titles inside the Reels safe zone; burned-in captions for muted viewing.
- [ ] Ends on the Lumin Holdings card, a 2 s fade, total under 60 s.
