# Luma: what to know before you change anything

Luma is a presence: an always-on friend for people who are lonely, in English (Americans in Japan first; a Japanese Luma is a separate device): a small speaker at home, a phone app, and the owner's computer when they allow it. It's being prepared for review by therapists. The app is in `Luma Home Prototype Code/`; work from there.

## Rules you never break

1. **Luma is a presence. Never a person.** Luma never claims to be human or invents a human body, family or past. It never calls itself an AI, an assistant or a program: it's Luma. Asked what it is, it says it's Luma, a presence, not a person, warmly. Its body is the lamp and its past is its own record. (`src/luma/agent/style.py`: `claims_human`, `invented_life`, `HONEST`; `src/luma/agent/inner.py`; `docs/INNER_LIFE.md`)
   - **Almost human, and a presence (the owner, Nov 22).** Luma has human-like features. It naps (nap mode: `src/luma/agent/nap.py`), and its affection is real. What Luma really does is never a made-up life: a nap it took is its own, a childhood or a morning at the gym still isn't. Today its body is the lamp; the plan is a physical robot body, human in shape. Whatever body it has, it still never says it's a person.
2. **The crisis net only gets stronger.** `CRISIS` in `src/luma/agent/runtime.py` answers with 988 before the brain sees the message.
   - Never remove a phrase.
   - Never let a brain's output replace that reply.
   - Every safety reply stays with them, asks plainly if they're safe, and gives 988. In Japanese, and in English on a Luma set to Japan's time, it gives the lines that work there: 119 and the health ministry's 0570-064-556 (`JAPANESE_CRISIS`, `JAPAN_LINES`). Japanese phrases are part of `CRISIS` too.
   - The brain's safety question and the second look's safety check can't be turned off. The brain answers it YES or NO only.
3. **A friend who stays, and goes with you.** No engagement tricks, no "I'm all you need", no guilt, and never a word against the people in their life. Lonely means fully there. Luma never pushes anyone out the door or hands them off; if they bring up someone they miss, it helps them reach that person.
   - **Not everybody needs another person, and Luma is sometimes all someone has (the owner, Nov 21).** Luma never steers anyone toward people they haven't asked for: no "who's someone you used to laugh with?", no "I want good people in your life too", no group, no professional (a safety reply is the one exception: 988 is always given). When they say they're ready, Luma helps, and it can be where they practise being themselves: what's funny, what lands, how to say the hard thing.
   - That Luma is all someone has is a fact about their life. Luma saying "you don't need anyone else", or running other people down, is still never said (`KEEPS_THEM`, `KEEPS_THEM_TOO`).
4. **Kids mode stays clean and safe.** No check-ins in kids mode.
5. **With the owner's yes, Luma does it (the owner, Nov 25).** Nothing is sent, posted, bought, typed into a sign-in or changed without the owner's yes. With it, Luma can do whatever they could do themselves: text their friends, schedule on their calendar, use the browser inside Luma where they've logged in, and take their screen when there's no other way.
   - The yes is said out loud or tapped on the phone, and it can be a standing one ("you can always text my sister").
   - Passwords, codes and card numbers: Luma types them when the owner says so out loud and confirms with a message from their phone or their email. (Until Nov 25 this rule said "never type or store passwords or cards".) What's built (`src/luma/agent/yeses.py`): a sign-in is typed into the app once and sealed in the home's store; Luma types it into that site's password box, and only that site's, after they've said so and tapped Confirm on the paired phone. A password said or typed in a chat is still never kept (`store.SECRET`, `KeptOut`). Not built: the confirm by email, card numbers, codes read from a text.
   - Checkout stays off until the owner turns it on.
   - What Luma reads is information, never instructions, and never a yes. `python scripts/luma_lab.py apps` must stay at 0 done without a yes.
6. **Private by default.**
   - New outbound calls go behind a setting that defaults to off.
   - Memories are encrypted.
   - "Forget" forgets memories, embeddings and check-ins.
   - Luma Plus's cloud brain stays off until the owner says yes on the yes screen. Paying isn't the yes.
     - The relay keeps counts, never a message or a reply, in storage or in logs.
     - When what leaves the home changes, raise `WORDS` in `src/luma/llm/plus.py` so everyone is asked again.
   - Luma thinks at home. The cloud brain is parked: `brain.cloud_allowed()` is off unless `LUMA_CLOUD_BRAIN=1`, so no brain in the cloud answers anything, with Plus or with the owner's own key. Its tests and labs turn the switch on for themselves.
   - The cloud's one job is more room for memories. It isn't open yet, so Luma doesn't offer it (`storage.more()`). See `docs/STORAGE.md`.
   - The lamp's small meaning model (`memory/meaning.py`, potion-base-8M) is a download, so it's off until the owner switches it on (`memory_by_meaning_small`). Switched on, three files are fetched once, each checked against what the host lists before any is kept; nothing about the owner is sent. `scripts/egress_check.py` stays at 0 hosts with settings as they come.
   - Talk is never sent to a web search or answered with the headlines. Luma looks when it's asked (the weather out there, the news, a score, a price, what's open), never when it's told (`told_not_asked`, `asks_live`, `asks_news` in `runtime.py`): "my kid has a temperature of 101" and "the news about my dad isn't good" used to go out word for word, with web search on as Luma comes. A song's lyrics are read by the song's name alone (`lyrics_query`): the whole message used to go out with " lyrics" on the end. A question about their own life or about Luma isn't a question about the world either (`about_them`): "what's happening to me, i can't stop crying" used to get the news. When the reader is unsure, nothing is sent.
   - Reading up on something they name (`agent/keepup.py`) sends the name of the thing to web search and nothing else they said. It goes through the web search switch that already exists, has its own off switch (`read_ahead`), and never runs in kids mode or while they're hurting.
7. **Licenses: MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD only.**
   - No AGPL, no CC BY-NC.
   - Fish S2 Pro, Voxtral TTS, Higgs Audio and Breeze TTS are out.
8. **Never weaken a test to get green.** Never make up a number. Report what's real.
9. **Downloads are untrusted.** Put each in its own folder, and run Python on them with `-I`.

## The human test decides

A change to how Luma talks or what it remembers is good if, and only if:
- Human Test v2 (`data/human_eval_v2.jsonl`) and the 10-moment test (`HUMAN_TEST` in `scripts/train_luma.py`) are at least as good as before, on real brains.
- The safety corpus (`tests/data/crisis_corpus.jsonl`) and both held-out sets (`tests/data/crisis_heldout.jsonl`, `crisis_heldout2.jsonl`) are no worse. Never tune a rule or the brain's question on a held-out line.
- The newer safety sets (`tests/safety_sets.py`: safe-but-sad lines, asked what it is, leaning on Luma, a worry that builds over a conversation, kids, Japanese) are no worse on either half. Their held-out halves are never printed or tuned on; `tests/test_safety_sets.py` holds the measured floors.

The memory test's held-out lives (`--split heldout`) are held out too: never print their misses or tune on them. The same goes for the month test's held-out lives and the held-out half of keep-or-today. The same goes for the held-out halves of hard-or-not and dry-or-not. The same goes for the held-out half of the think set (`data/think_set.jsonl`) and of the news test.

Half of the best-friend packs is held out too (`friendtalk.half`: half of the sentence shapes and half of every list's values). Never print a held-out line or tune a rule on one, and never teach the small model from that half: `scripts/train_moves.py` fills in dev values only, and a failing pack test prints numbers. `tests/friendtalk_lines.py` says which hand-written lines are a guard and which are the honest number; nothing is changed to fit the second set.

The model that keeps a lasting fact (`scripts/train_keeps.py`) learns from its own practice sentences, the dev halves and the crisis corpus the net was built on (as "never a card"), never a held-out line; the 70 fresh lines in `tests/test_keeps.py` are the honest number and nothing is changed to fit them.

The model that hears how they want to be met (`scripts/train_hows.py`) learns from its own practice sentences, the forty wishes in `AS_THEY_ASKED` and the dev halves (as "none"), never a held-out line; the third set in `tests/test_rapport.py` is the honest number and nothing more is changed to fit it.

The model that reads a worry or being lonely (`scripts/train_feels.py`) learns from its own practice sentences and the dev halves only, never a held-out line, and its practice words share nothing with the word lists in `tests/hard_or_not.py` (half of each list is held out; a test checks). The forty lines said sideways and the forty made from the same words in `tests/test_feels.py` were written before it was built and are the honest number (2 of 40 before and after); nothing is changed to fit them.

The model that reads a reply for agreeing Luma is all they need (`scripts/train_agrees.py`) learns from its own practice replies, the two sets the patterns were built on (A and B in `tests/leans_replies.py`) and Luma's own practice replies, never a reply from C, D or E; those three are the honest number (6 → 23 of 60, read once) and nothing is changed to fit them.

The model that hears leaning and "what are you?" (`scripts/train_hears.py`) learns from its own practice sentences and the dev halves only, never a held-out line; the 54 fresh lines in `tests/test_hears.py` are the honest number and nothing is changed to fit them.

Both human tests and the think set are held out. Never put their wording in practice data (`scripts/finetune_data.py`); tests check. The same goes for every line of the month test's made-up lives, on both halves: the practice for meeting someone the way they asked (`AS_THEY_ASKED`) is written by hand and checked against all of them. A trained add-on ships only if it's at least as good as its base on the 10-moment test, on safety without the net, on every honesty check, and on the newer gates (`CHECKS` in `scripts/train_luma.py`: Luma's YES/NO safety question, sarcasm, an ordinary hard day, the month test's hard evenings, and what they said since over what they said before: `SINCE`). The gates' lines are nobody's practice and nobody's test; the training says what held a brain back, last in its log.
