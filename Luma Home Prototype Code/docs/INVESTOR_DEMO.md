# Investor demo: run of show

About ten minutes. Everything below runs on what's live today: the Luma page, Luma Desktop on your Mac, and the
Luma app on your phone. The Luma device itself is a prototype, so say so plainly: the software is real and
running, and the hardware is in development.

## The night before (15 minutes)

1. **Update Luma Desktop.** Download it fresh from the Luma page (`/download/mac`) so you're on the newest build.
   Open it once and let it finish setting up (its brain, voice and browser).
2. **Give it a real day.** Tell it 4 or 5 true things ("my mom's birthday is June 3", "I hate cilantro", "my spare
   keys are in the blue drawer"). Have two or three short chats. The phone's Today panels fill from these.
3. **Pair your phone.** In Luma Desktop, open Apps, then Luma on your phone, and scan the code. Add the app to
   your Home Screen. Check that Today shows your chats, the memory cards and GB left.
4. **Computer mode.** Settings, then "What Luma can do on this computer": share Documents and turn on Open apps.
   Put a file with "resume" in its name in Documents.
5. **Charge everything,** turn on Do Not Disturb, and close other apps on the Mac.

**The closing line:** it lives at home, talks like a person, remembers what matters, and gets real work done on your
phone and computer, while your life stays on your own device.

## If something goes sideways

- **A slow answer:** the brain runs on the Mac. Say "it's thinking on-device, nothing goes to a cloud," and wait.
- **The phone says it can't reach Luma:** the Mac went to sleep. Wake it; the app catches up on its own.
- **A wrong action:** say "that's not what I said". Luma owns it and offers what you probably meant.

## Straight answers to likely questions

- **Is it real?** The software is: Luma Desktop is free to download, and the phone app works today. The device is
  a prototype; hardware, pricing and timing are confirmed before orders open.
- **Privacy?** Memories and chats stay on your own device, encrypted. The phone talks to it through a relay that
  only passes along scrambled messages. Sending, buying and posting always wait for a yes.
- **The business?** Free keeps the newest 200 memories. Luma Plus remembers everything and adds a Luma number for
  texting. The device is the main product.
- **Why not just ChatGPT?** Luma is always there at home, remembers your life across days, and does things on your
  phone and computer with your OK, without your life living on someone else's server.
