# Critique, October 25 2026: Luma, and the work on it

A harsh look at where Luma stands after the "real human" and design pass, and at what was done this round.

## What changed this round

- **Talks like you.** Luma now has a slang lexicon: about 500 entries and 900 spellings from 25 communities,
  each entry with its meaning and origin. It also catches stretched words ("brooo", "slayyy") and simple
  endings. From your own messages it builds a picture of how you talk: your words, how much you cuss, how long
  you write, and which community your slang comes from. It mirrors that register, and only that one. Luma never
  pulls in a dialect you don't use and never puts on an accent.
- **Knows where words come from.** Ask "what does bussin mean" and you get the meaning and the origin (Black
  American English). Teach it ("zorp means cool but weird") and it remembers.
- **More words, legally.** `scripts/build_slang.py` grows the lexicon from Wiktionary's slang labels (CC BY-SA)
  on your own computer.
- **Characters.** Say "talk like a pirate", "be my hype man", "roleplay as a medieval knight" and so on. There
  are 16 built-in characters, plus anything you name. A character sticks until you say "be yourself". For a real
  person or a fictional character, Luma does an impression of the vibe: no claiming to be them, no reciting
  their lines.
- **Funny on purpose.** There's a humor guide (specific, callbacks, no canned jokes, never forced). Lines that
  made you laugh are saved as running jokes (the last twelve) so Luma can call back to one now and then.
- **Unhinged mode.** Adults only, and only when you ask. It means full cussing, real roasts and hot takes.
  Turn it on in chat ("turn on unhinged mode"; it checks the birthday you gave at sign-up) or in Settings. Turn
  it off with "tone it down". Luma's voice says "wtf" out loud the way you meant it.
- **Never a slur.** No mode and no model makes Luma say one. Replies and speech are cleaned before anyone sees
  or hears them. The casual n-word becomes "bro".
- **Websites with taste.** The website studio was rebuilt from four design skills. Each site gets:
  - one of six art directions, with the reason written in the stylesheet
  - no gradient blobs, eyebrow labels, pill-everything or hover lifts
  - real brand art in the hero instead of filler
  - light and dark modes, visible keyboard focus, reduced motion and 44px tap targets
  - gates that send the copy back for buzzwords, generic buttons and long headlines

  Numbers and quotes with no source are left out, and the step says so.
- **Brand graphics.** The logo is flat (no gradient). Posters use big type, not blobs. The app mockup shows real
  words, never grey placeholder bars.
- **A brain for the Mac Studio.** On 128 GB of memory or more, "Get the studio brain" downloads the biggest open
  model that fits, in all its pieces, each checked: Qwen3.5 397B-A17B first, then Qwen3 235B-A22B. It runs on
  the same engine as today's brains.

## Luma, harshly

1. **A small brain can't be reliably funny.** The default brain is Qwen3 4B. Prompts get it close, but a 4B
   model overdoes slang, repeats bits and misses timing. The prompt is also long (identity, humor, your
   register, character, jokes, news), and small models lose instructions in long prompts. Fix: the 30B on 32 GB
   Macs, the studio brain on a Mac Studio, or the cloud brain. Then run `scripts/luma_check.py` and read every
   reply.
2. **None of this round was tested on a real model.** The build machine had no language model and couldn't
   download one. The tests prove the wiring: the register notes, characters, jokes, unhinged mode and slur
   cleaning reach the brain and come back right. They don't prove the replies are good. That's the first thing
   to do on your Mac.
3. **The voice is still the weakest link.** Kokoro is clean, but it sounds read rather than talked. The
   expressive engines (Chatterbox-Turbo, Qwen3-TTS) can laugh and breathe and are wired in, but they're still in
   progress. Real people also overlap and backchannel ("mm-hm", "wait wait"), and Luma takes turns. On a Mac
   Studio, make the expressive engine the default and work on barge-in.
4. **Slang coverage is honest, not 100,000.** The real, current slang in use is a few thousand terms. A
   100,000-entry list would be mostly obsolete, joke or invented entries, and it would make Luma worse. The long
   tail comes from the model itself, the Wiktionary import and what you teach it. Slang moves monthly, and
   nothing updates the curated list automatically yet.
5. **Unhinged mode depends on the model playing along.** Small local models sometimes sanitize. The cloud
   brain may soften harsh language. The slur filter is a pattern list: it catches the common spellings, not
   every creative one.
6. **Characters follow you everywhere.** A character set in the app also shapes replies by text message.
   That's probably right, but it could surprise someone. A per-channel setting would be cleaner.
7. **Luma's own app breaks some of the rules it now enforces.** There's glass on many surfaces, a few
   letter-spaced section labels ("CHATS") and the "S.I." badge. They stay because you chose that look, and
   that's recorded in DESIGN.md as a deliberate exception, but they're worth a second look.
8. **The live website wasn't touched.** The Luma S.I. site lives in `mtsjay123-sudo/lumin-holdings-site`. This
   session wasn't allowed to add that repo, so the redesign with these rules still needs doing there.
9. **Generated sites still lack real photos.** The six directions and the brand art beat filler, but a
   restaurant needs its food and a studio needs its work. There's no image-generation model and no photo search
   with licenses yet.

## The work, harshly

- **Quality went unproven.** Tests checked that things are wired up, not that Luma sounds human. A transcript
  from a real model would have been better evidence. It wasn't possible here, and that's stated rather than
  papered over.
- **Model picks rest on search results.** HuggingFace was blocked from the build machine, so the repo names
  and licenses for the studio brain couldn't be confirmed directly. The code skips any repo that isn't there,
  but check the license on the model card before relying on it commercially.
- **The design judgment is one reviewer's eye.** The six directions were judged by screenshot. Run a blind
  side-by-side with real people.
- **Two bugs shipped silently last round** (the project page crash and unstyled previews). Screenshots caught
  them. Every UI change gets a screenshot before it ships.
- **Some requests were declined:**
  - **Scraping TikTok, Instagram and X comments and transcripts to learn slang.** It breaks those platforms'
    rules and takes people's words without asking. Luma learns slang from Wiktionary, from its own lexicon and
    from you instead.
  - **Training on creators' videos and voices.** Declined earlier, for the same reasons plus voice-likeness
    laws. A paid, opt-in voice drive with releases is still on offer.
